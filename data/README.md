# Data provenance

`gil_castell_2019_pcl_mn.csv` transcribes the PCL rows of Table 3 in:

Gil-Castell O, Badia JD, Bou J, Ribes-Greus A. *Performance of Polyester-Based Electrospun Scaffolds under In Vitro Hydrolytic Conditions: From Short-Term to Long-Term Applications.* Nanomaterials. 2019;9(5):786. DOI: `10.3390/nano9050786`.

The article is open access. Values are number-average molecular weights for electrospun PCL scaffolds immersed at 37 °C in ultra-pure water or PBS. Units were converted from g/mol to kDa (numerically division by 1000). No values were digitized from figures or imputed.

The validation workflow uses the water observations at 0--400 days for calibration and reserves 500 and 650 days as a pre-declared temporal holdout. The CSV contains no measurement uncertainties because the source table does not report them for these entries; unweighted residual metrics are therefore used.
